Fire Island National Seashore

Exploring Fire Island is an unforgettable coastal escape filled with pristine sandy beaches, untouched maritime forests, and rich wildlife. From hiking along peaceful boardwalks to taking in breathtaking Atlantic sunset views, this park offers a rare slice of natural serenity.
